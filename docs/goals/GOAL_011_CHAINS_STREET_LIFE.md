## D. SUPPLY/REUP: MULTIPLE PACKS, PLUGS, DEBTS

Currently `StreetEconomy.supplierError` globally forbids additional stock with an open debt/lot, and `saleLot` / `StreetSessions` assume the first live lot. **Replace these assumptions with genuine multi-lot support** while preserving old saves and Quanny consignment.

- Player may seek more stock **while already holding unsold stock** and/or still owing money. They may reup from the same supplier or obtain work from a different authorized plug.
- For any plug other than Mega, willingness to supply before previous repayment depends on **their own directional trust, existing debts to that plug, recent payment history, risk, inventory/resource capacity, and access**. Low trust gives a clear “Come back when you pay me what you owe” response. A debt to Plug A is not mistakenly charged to Plug B or treated as automatically granting Plug B trust. Even when declining, NPC remains consistent with their established relationship.
- **Mega always agrees to supply his cousin unless they have become actual enemies**. He is not blocked by merely holding stock, a previous pending payment or a generic global resupply cooldown. Normal transactional AP/stock-budget rules and explicit correct cash/credit terms still apply. **If the player initially DECLINED aiding Mega, Mega supplies on CASH terms ONLY, with no consignment**. Avoid imposing a cash-only restriction on a player who actually promised help, unless individual relationship consequences justify it.
- Every lot keeps unique ID, original supplier, acquisition terms, quantity received/sold/lost, gross, own debt agreement, issue date, due date, loss/repayment records, ownership and provenance. Existing six-day agreement timers remain independent per pack; no accidental due-date reset when taking another lot. Repayment UI lets player pick **which supplier/agreement** to settle, including part-payments. NPC collection/default checks iterate all applicable agreements, not just first.
- Inventory supports multiple held lots and displays aggregate units plus inspectable supplier-level lots, balances and deadlines. Player must be able to **select a real stock lot** before starting a board session or get a clear deterministic default (earliest due date, stable lot ID), with visible selection and no wrong-supplier debits. Session persists selected lot and continues consuming that exact lot, not dynamically switching when inventory changes; handle depletion safely. Cash/exchanges and Trapper XP represent real per-buyer transactions only.
- Quanny's consignment of player's stock remains **Quanny owes the player**; multiple suppliers the player owes are **the opposite direction**. Never conflate the ledgers. Maintain present Quanny collection, 6-day commitments and disputes. Existing job income, clean/illicit wallets and event log remain accurate.
- Gate early supplier requests via tutorial state, but not by old “finish current lot” restrictions after lesson. No infinite free stock, credit exploit, debt laundering or repeated pack acquisition via stale taps.

Use reasonable prices, stock and clear requirements; no generic “purchase preparation” menu. Consumables are game inventory objects, consumed or lost as defined, saved and shown in exchange feed. No simulation or real-world instructions for bypassing actual alarms or locks.
3. **Travel physically to the job location** and initiate the specified robbery type, validated on the board, with enough AP, actual opportunity, required prep items, Combat rank, correct equipped weapon if mandatory and optionally eligible/willing NPC help.
4. **Active event/session**: 1 AP charged ONCE on start. Present multi-beat board activity with character movement/alerts and at least one meaningful player decision or **abstract, clearly fictional puzzle** (timing/memory/pattern) where appropriate. Example mechanic is a fictional timed tile-sequence challenge, not a realistic safe or alarm system tutorial. 5. **Outcome**: success moves actual defined loot to wallet/inventory, applies heat, victim/witness/organizational memory and Robbery XP; real failure can yield no loot, arrest, injury or increased heat/hostility depending on context. Neither successful nor failed robbery automatically requires someone dying. Use the existing danger and CriminalExposure systems, including fatality limitations. Distinguish partial outcomes where appropriate.
6. **Exit to City** in the actual location with remaining AP (unless incarcerated, injured, or interrupted). Repeat attempts require a new eligible job; already resolved opportunity cannot be farmed. Save/load should not reroll outcomes or grant cash twice.

**Type progression:** Intro **strong-arm** first, then home invasion, store robbery, and bank/major score as future tiers. Build data-driven type definitions, validation and event session architecture for all types, but only **author and make actually playable the introductory suitable lower-tier opportunity plus a coherent follow-on home-invasion opportunity** in this GOAL if it can be presented without violating rank access. Ensure routes from qualified trio business and Quanny (or another canonically justified source) support reaching the introduction, including players who oppose the trio. **Robbery safeguards:** Same persisted state-machine design principles as trapping (separate `activeRobbery` or compatible discriminated activity type; never competing active sessions, no off-board initiation, immutable replay seeds, event cursor/ack IDs, one-time AP, stock/loot invariants, explicit save migration).
