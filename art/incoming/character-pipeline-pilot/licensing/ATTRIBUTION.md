# Pilot asset attribution

**Motion capture** — “100STYLE Dataset,” Neutral_ID, Neutral_FW and Neutral_FR, by Ian Mason, Sebastian Starke and Taku Komura. Distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The pilot crops, aligns, samples at 30 Hz, retargets, loops and adjusts the capture for in-place motion and camera-facing gaze. The resulting animation appears in the pilot Blender scenes, GLBs and videos. [Publisher’s data page](https://www.ianxmason.com/100style/) · [Dataset DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8127870)

**MPFB anatomy and graphics** — MakeHuman Community, CC0 1.0 core graphics and system assets. The experiment uses the MPFB basemesh, fitted game-engine bones and skin weights, macro targets, young_african_male skin and low-poly brown eyes. The Blender add-on and asset archives are external build inputs. [MakeHuman licensing](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html) · [CC0 system pack](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html)

**Hood Hero materials** — Existing protagonist, outfit, textures and art belong to their existing repository provenance. The pilot includes owner-authorized comparison copies and derivatives. [Protagonist delivery](https://github.com/kalikeefbiz/Hood-Hero-Lab/pull/71) · [Wardrobe delivery](https://github.com/kalikeefbiz/Hood-Hero-Lab/pull/77)
